  • Who I Am

    I’m a software engineer and system architect who builds reliable digital systems for organizations where correctness, security, and long-term maintainability matter.

    I specialize in designing and evolving backend-heavy applications — platforms that handle money, sensitive data, complex workflows, and real operational constraints. My work spans greenfield builds and legacy modernization, with a strong focus on systems that need to survive production, not just demos.

    I don’t chase trends. I design systems that are understandable, auditable, and resilient — systems that teams can maintain and businesses can trust.

  • What I Do

    I design, build, and modernize software systems with a strong emphasis on backend architecture and system integrity.

    This includes:
    • High-reliability backend systems (APIs, transactional workflows, data-heavy platforms)
    • Payment and financial integrations where correctness and idempotency are non-negotiable
    • Legacy system modernization, migrating existing platforms to modern stacks without breaking production
    • Architecture consulting, helping teams untangle complexity and make sustainable technical decisions
    • Hybrid architectures (BFFs, serverless + traditional backends) that balance performance, security, and delivery speed

    I work closely with clients to translate business requirements into technical systems that scale with both usage and organizational growth.

  • Why I Do It

    Most costly software failures don’t come from bad ideas — they come from fragile systems, rushed architecture, and hidden technical debt.

    I build software because I care about systems that hold up under pressure: systems that behave correctly when traffic spikes, when money is involved, when requirements change, and when the original developers are no longer around.

    I enjoy solving the hard parts of engineering — state, concurrency, data integrity, and evolution over time — because getting those right early saves clients years of pain, rewrites, and unexpected costs later.

Skills & Tools

Database Design

I design databases as systems, not just storage layers.

My focus is on data models that reflect real business constraints, enforce integrity at the database level, and remain performant as complexity grows. I’m particularly attentive to transaction boundaries, indexing strategies, and query behavior under load.

I’m comfortable working close to the metal when necessary (raw SQL, locking strategies, query plans), and equally comfortable introducing ORMs. The goal is always the same: predictable performance, clear data ownership, and schemas that can evolve without breaking production.

Core strengths: Relational Modeling, Transactional Integrity, Indexing Strategy, Concurrency Control, Data Migrations.

Backend Development

Back-end engineering is where I do my best work.

I build APIs and backend systems with a strong emphasis on correctness, security, and long-term maintainability. Business logic lives in clearly defined layers, independent of frameworks, making systems easier to test, reason about, and scale.

I pay close attention to system boundaries: authentication vs authorization, reads vs writes, synchronous vs asynchronous flows. Whether it’s handling payments, managing distributed state, or modernizing a legacy system, I design backends to behave correctly under real-world conditions — not just ideal ones.

If given the opportunity to specialize, this is where I would focus: designing robust, production-grade backend systems that businesses can rely on.

Core strengths: API Design, Authentication & Authorization, Transactional Workflows, System Architecture.

Frontend Development

While I can build any kind of UI, I enjoy building data-driven interfaces that prioritize clarity, correctness, and usability.

My strongest front-end work lives in admin panels, dashboards, and internal tools — interfaces where users need to understand complex data, perform precise actions, and trust the system. This is where I find frontend work most interesting — and where thoughtful design has the highest impact.

I pay close attention to data fetching patterns, managing states, and error handling, ensuring the UI accurately reflects backend state at all times. Queries, mutations, and cache invalidation are designed deliberately so the interface stays responsive without becoming fragile or overcomplicated.

Core strengths: Data-Heavy UI Design, Admin Dashboards, Structured Layouts, Predictable State Management, Clean and Maintainable Front-end Architecture.
